# subtitle.im

**YouTube captions, on when needed, off when they get in the way.**

[Website](https://subtitle.im/en/) · [Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ehlfcnlpbeoncpoolmnocnjffmohhdbl) · [Help](https://subtitle.im/help/) · [Report a problem or share feedback](https://github.com/zenoven/subtitle.im/issues/new/choose) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

subtitle.im is a Chrome extension for managing YouTube's existing captions. It enables available captions and uses local OCR to detect subtitles burned into the video, then handles overlapping captions according to your preferences. You can restore captions whenever you need them.

## What you can do

- Automatically enable available YouTube captions.
- Handle duplicate captions when the video already has burned-in subtitles.
- When the languages differ, choose to close captions, ask first, or keep them.
- Restore captions from the player; manual CC changes take priority for the current viewing.
- With Pro, use channel rules, individual video exceptions, and memory of confirmed caption closures.

Free use needs no sign-up and includes 10 successfully handled videos per installation. See the [website](https://subtitle.im/en/#pricing) for current Pro plans.

Recognition runs locally in your browser without uploading video frames or subtitle text for detection. Account and purchase services use a separate online connection.

The extension manages switchable YouTube captions. It does not generate, translate or download subtitles, or remove burned-in subtitles. Detection can miss subtitles or produce false positives; restore captions if needed.
